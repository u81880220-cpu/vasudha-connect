Delete your KAMPRO account

Account deletion information

You can request deletion of your KAMPRO account and associated personal information. This page is intended for customers and professionals who have created a KAMPRO account.

How to request deletion

  1. Open KAMPRO and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Use the account or profile settings to look for the delete-account option, if it is available in your app version.
  3. If you cannot access an in-app deletion option, contact KAMPRO through the support/contact channel shown in the app or the developer contact listed on the app’s Google Play Store page. Ask for “KAMPRO account deletion” and provide the mobile number linked to the account.

What happens after a request

We will verify that the request comes from the account holder before processing it. Account and profile information will be deleted or de-identified where applicable, subject to legal, security, fraud-prevention, transaction-record and dispute-resolution requirements. Certain records may be retained for the period required by law.

Before you submit

Please do not include your password, OTP, full card number or UPI PIN in a deletion request. If you have an active job or unresolved payment, mention it so the request can be handled appropriately.

Launch requirement: KAMPRO must add a monitored support email or a working deletion-request form and confirm the in-app account deletion flow before publishing this page as the final Google Play account-deletion URL.
