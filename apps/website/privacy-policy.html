Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how KAMPRO (“KAMPRO”, “we”, “us” or “our”) may collect, use, store and share information when you use the KAMPRO mobile application or website. KAMPRO is a platform intended to help customers discover service professionals and connect with them.

1. Information we may collect

  • Account and profile details: name, mobile number, account identifiers, profile photo and the details you choose to add.
  • Professional details: services offered, service area, availability, work portfolio, experience and documents you submit for identity or skill verification.
  • Location: location you select or allow the app to access, including approximate or current location when you use location-based discovery. Location is used to show relevant nearby professionals and service areas.
  • Service and communication data: connection requests, job details, messages, ratings, reviews and support communications.
  • Payments: package or subscription purchase details and transaction references. Payment credentials are handled by the payment provider where applicable; KAMPRO does not intend to collect full card or UPI credentials.
  • Technical information: device, app version, diagnostic and usage information needed to operate, secure and improve the service.

2. How we use information

We use information to create and manage accounts, display professional profiles, match services and locations, enable customer-professional connections, process applicable package or subscription transactions, support verification, prevent misuse, provide customer support, maintain security and improve KAMPRO.

3. Location and profile visibility

Location is used to support nearby discovery. A professional’s exact residential or live GPS coordinates should not be displayed publicly to customers; the app may show an approximate location or service area instead. Information you add to a public professional profile may be visible to other users.

4. When information may be shared

We may share relevant information with another user when needed to provide a requested connection (for example, profile and contact details after a customer unlocks a connection). We may also use service providers for hosting, analytics, communications, security and payment processing, and disclose information when required by law or necessary to protect users and the platform. We do not sell personal information to advertisers.

5. Storage and security

We use technical and organisational measures intended to protect account and service data. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Access to sensitive verification documents should be limited to authorised purposes.

6. Retention

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the service, meet legal and accounting obligations, resolve disputes, enforce our terms and maintain security. Retention periods may differ for account data, transaction records, communications and verification documents.

7. Your choices and rights

You may review and update profile information through the app where those controls are available. You may request account deletion using the instructions on our Delete Account page. Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security, fraud-prevention or dispute-resolution purposes.

8. Children

KAMPRO is not intended for children under 18 to create independent accounts or use professional marketplace features. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact KAMPRO so the matter can be reviewed.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as KAMPRO changes. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.

10. Contact

Before public launch, KAMPRO will publish a monitored support contact on this page. For now, please use the support/contact option shown inside the app, if available. Do not send passwords, OTPs or full payment credentials in a support request.

Publication note: This is a launch draft. The operator’s legal name, support email, final data-retention periods, subprocessors and production payment-provider details must be confirmed before this policy is submitted as the final public policy.
