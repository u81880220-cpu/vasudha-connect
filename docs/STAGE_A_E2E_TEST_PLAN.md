# VASUDHA CONNECT — Stage A Free E2E Test Plan\n\n## Demo accounts\n\nThe repository contains `apps/mobile/scripts/create-demo-accounts.mjs`.\n\nRun it from a trusted development machine with the Supabase project URL and secret/service-role key supplied through environment variables. Never commit the key or put it in the Expo app.\n\nIt creates four QA identities:\n\n| Role | Email | Password |\n|---|---|---|\n| Customer 1 | demo.customer1@vasudha.test | Demo@12345 |\n| Customer 2 | demo.customer2@vasudha.test | Demo@12345 |\n| Professional 1 | demo.pro1@vasudha.test | Demo@12345 |\n| Professional 2 | demo.pro2@vasudha.test | Demo@12345 |\n\nProfessional 1 is an AC Technician. Professional 2 is a Carpenter.\nBoth professionals are seeded as verified, available, with a 25 km Varanasi service area. Both customers receive 10 test connection credits.\n\nThese are QA-only identities and must be removed before production launch.\n\n## Stage A flow\n\n### A. Customer onboarding\n1. Create/login as a fresh customer.\n2. Confirm the first-time route asks only for basic name/contact information.\n3. Continue to Home.\n4. Open marketplace without selecting a category/sub-service.\n5. Search `AC`.\n6. Search `carpenter`.\n7. Search `plumber`.\n8. Open a professional profile.\n9. Confirm phone is hidden before connection.\n\n### B. Professional onboarding\n1. Create/login as a fresh professional.\n2. Enter basic profile.\n3. Select one or more services.\n4. Select General / Any or a specific sub-service.\n5. Set Varanasi service area and radius.\n6. Submit verification.\n7. Confirm status is visible.\n8. Confirm a verified/active professional can become available.\n\n### C. Connection\n1. Customer opens Demo AC Professional.\n2. Unlock/connect.\n3. Confirm connection balance decreases by one.\n4. Confirm professional phone is available only after connection.\n5. Test Call button.\n6. Test VASUDHA chat.\n\n### D. Direct job\n1. Customer enters job title and description.\n2. Add preferred date/time.\n3. Add service location.\n4. Submit.\n5. Professional receives the request.\n6. Before acceptance, confirm customer phone and exact service location are hidden.\n7. Professional accepts.\n8. Confirm customer phone and exact service location/map are now available.\n9. Confirm professional becomes unavailable for another active job.\n\n### E. Job lifecycle\nExecute exactly:\n`worker_accepted → on_the_way → arrived → work_started → work_completed → customer_confirmed`\n\nAt each step verify correct UI state, database state, notification/in-app notification, and no unauthorized data exposure.\nAfter `work_completed`, verify the professional becomes available again.\n\n### F. After completion\n1. Customer rates professional.\n2. Professional rates customer.\n3. Verify reputation changes.\n4. Customer taps Rehire Professional.\n5. Confirm a new service request is created with the previous job context.\n6. Submit a controlled complaint.\n7. Confirm complaint status/history is visible.\n8. Change complaint status from admin and verify participant notification.\n\n## Privacy/RLS checks\n\n- Public professional profile does not expose phone.\n- Customer cannot read another customer's private phone.\n- Professional cannot read customer phone/location before acceptance.\n- Professional can read accepted-job contact/location only for the relevant job.\n- Customer cannot manipulate another customer's or professional's jobs.\n- A professional cannot accept a request belonging to another professional.\n- A busy professional cannot accept another active job.\n- Connection unlock is customer-only and consumes only the authenticated customer's credits.\n- Rehire is customer-only and only available after a completed/confirmed job.\n\n## External integrations intentionally deferred\n\nStage A does not require paid production providers.\nLater production checks: real SMS/OTP delivery, production push delivery on physical Android, production routing/ETA provider, and payment gateway.\nMap UI, coordinates, privacy rules, job lifecycle, and database/RLS are testable before those paid integrations.
