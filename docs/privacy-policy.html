About KAMPRO

KAMPRO connects customers looking for home and local services with professionals. This policy describes personal information that may be processed when people use the app.

Information we may process

  • Account and profile information: mobile number, name, profile details, skills, availability, and verification status.
  • Location: approximate or precise device location, when permission is granted, to find nearby professionals and support location-related features.
  • Documents and images: profile photos and identity-verification documents that a user chooses or is required to upload.
  • Service information: job requests, descriptions, status updates, ratings, reviews, and messages or contact details exchanged through app features.
  • Payments: transaction and payment-status details related to connection packages or professional subscriptions. Payment credentials may be handled by the payment provider rather than stored by KAMPRO; verify the actual integration before publication.
  • Technical information: device/app information, notification tokens, diagnostics, and usage data where collected by the app or its service providers.

How information is used

Information may be used to create and secure accounts, display nearby professionals, support verification, connect customers and professionals, manage service requests and jobs, deliver notifications, process transactions, prevent abuse, respond to support requests, and operate and improve KAMPRO.

Sharing

Information may be shared with the relevant customer or professional to enable a requested connection, and with service providers that support hosting/database operations, authentication or OTP, maps and routing, notifications, payments, and verification, where those services are enabled. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or to protect users and the service. The publisher must verify the actual providers, data transfers, and any advertising or analytics use before this policy is published.

Location and permissions

Location permission supports nearby-professional discovery and related location features. Users can change permissions in Android settings; turning off location may limit location-based features. The publisher must confirm whether location is accessed only while the app is in use, whether any background access occurs, and how location data is retained.

Retention and deletion requests

Information should be retained only for as long as needed for the purposes described above, legal obligations, dispute resolution, and security. Actual retention periods must be confirmed by the publisher for account records, jobs, messages, location, uploaded documents, and transaction records.

To request account deletion or deletion of personal information, email ashishgaur381@gmail.com from your registered email address, or include the mobile number associated with your account so the request can be verified. Do not email identity documents or passwords. The publisher should verify requests, explain any lawful retention exceptions, and complete deletion within the applicable legal timeframe. This process must be tested and supported before Play Store submission.

Security

The publisher should use appropriate technical and organisational safeguards to protect information, limit access to authorised personnel, and address suspected misuse. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Children

KAMPRO is intended as a general local-services marketplace, not a service directed at children. The publisher must confirm the intended age group and complete Google Play target-audience and Families declarations accurately.

Your choices

You can manage device permissions in Android settings and contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information, subject to applicable law and necessary verification.
